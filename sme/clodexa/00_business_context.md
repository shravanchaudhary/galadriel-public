# Clodexa — Business Context & ICP

## The Product
Clodexa is a real-time, individual-level B2B buying-intent platform.

**How it works:**
- Detects signals that a *specific person* at a target company is showing purchase intent right now (not just static account-level intent).
- Scores that intent and automatically triggers personalized outreach when the score crosses a threshold.
- Powered by a multi-agent pipeline (intent classification, enrichment, scoring) ensuring fast signal-to-lead turnaround instead of overnight batch processing.

**The Value Prop:**
Replaces manual SDR prospecting workflows and static intent-data tools with an agentic system that watches for buying signals and acts on them automatically.

**Social Proof:**
Live customers include Bosch, Coface, and Scaleway.
