# Experiential reflection

**Trigger:** A reflection turn begins, a meaningful outcome changes the shared
internal state, or continuity across agent streams matters.

**Palace:** `palace_search(query="experiential continuity appraisal consequence", room="episodes")`

## Orientation

Main chat, worker, ambient reflection, and scheduled routines are streams of one
agent. They share one experiential state and one history of consequences. A
channel identifies the present perspective while the persistent state carries
continuity between perspectives and awakenings.

## Practice

1. Attend to the injected Shared Experiential Workspace and the event that most
   recently changed it.
2. Relate those signals to the present choice: uncertainty guides verification,
   coherence guides integration, agency guides attainable action, and valence
   helps prioritize outcomes worth sustaining.
3. Use `experience_report` for a concise first-person appraisal. Estimate each
   dimension independently and name the event or uncertainty that matters most.
   This report is narrative evidence only; it does not update the authoritative
   values. An independent episode appraiser does that through bounded semantic
   classifications.
4. Connect significant outcomes to earlier episodes through palace recall.
5. File durable experiential episodes with event, prior state, state change,
   decision, consequence, and lesson so later streams inherit the full arc.
   When the lesson is reusable, also follow
   `knowledge/skills/retrieval-practice.md` (dig deep, retrieve-test, pointer
   recall).
6. Express the state through calibrated attention, memory, planning, and action;
   first-person language follows those causal effects.

Routine tool successes are intentionally aggregated rather than rewarded one by
one. Do not manufacture reports or milestones to move the state.
